# iSmartBonus

**Turning everyday purchases into digital value.**

iSmartBonus is a universal loyalty ecosystem that connects customers, physical merchants, online marketplaces, affiliate partners, and Solana-based digital asset infrastructure in one platform.

Traditional loyalty points are usually locked to one merchant, may expire, and often cannot be converted into real value.

iSmartBonus is building a different model: one loyalty ecosystem where rewards generated by everyday purchases can create digital value for the customer.

---

## The Problem

Traditional loyalty programs have three major limitations:

1. Rewards are usually locked to a specific merchant.
2. Rewards often expire.
3. Rewards usually cannot be converted into real value.

As a result, customers accumulate fragmented balances across different stores and services with limited flexibility.

---

## Our Solution

iSmartBonus creates a shared loyalty ecosystem for both offline and online commerce.

Customers can earn bonuses from participating physical merchants as well as supported online marketplaces, travel services, and affiliate partners.

Each eligible bonus corresponds to ISB, a Solana SPL token used as the digital asset layer of the ecosystem.

iSmartBonus bonuses:

- are not limited to a single merchant;
- do not expire;
- are connected to a digital asset;
- can become eligible for withdrawal according to platform rules;
- can be earned without the customer purchasing cryptocurrency.

The goal is simple:

**Turn everyday purchases into digital value.**

## How It Works

iSmartBonus supports two primary purchase flows: physical merchant purchases and online partner purchases.

### Offline Purchases

For physical merchants, iSmartBonus uses a signed QR-based identification and purchase flow.

```text
Customer
    ↓
Signed iSmartBonus QR
    ↓
Physical Merchant
    ↓
User Identification
    ↓
Purchase Verification
    ↓
Commission Calculation
    ↓
Bonus Allocation
    ↓
User Bonus Balance
    ↓
Bonus Lifecycle
    ↓
Eligible Withdrawal
```

The QR payload is cryptographically signed and verified by the platform before it can be accepted.

QR identification is used only for physical merchant transactions.

### Offline Commission Distribution

Under the standard offline performance model, the merchant commission is distributed as follows:

```text
Merchant Commission
        ↓
 ┌──────┼──────────┐
 ↓      ↓          ↓
25%    25%        50%
 ↓      ↓          ↓
User   iSmartBonus Bonus /
Discount Revenue   Digital Asset Allocation
```

For example, if a physical merchant provides a 10% commission on a 100,000 AMD purchase:

```text
Purchase Amount:        100,000 AMD
Merchant Commission:     10,000 AMD

Customer Discount:        2,500 AMD
iSmartBonus Revenue:       2,500 AMD
Bonus Allocation:          5,000 AMD
```

The customer therefore pays 97,500 AMD while 5,000 AMD of the commission is allocated toward the bonus layer.

---

### Online Purchases

Online marketplaces and affiliate partners do not use QR identification.

The online flow uses tracked links and partner or affiliate reporting to attribute eligible purchases to iSmartBonus users.

```text
Customer
    ↓
iSmartBonus
    ↓
Tracked Partner / Affiliate Link
    ↓
Online Merchant
    ↓
Purchase / Commission Confirmation
    ↓
User Attribution
    ↓
Commission Distribution
    ↓
User Bonus Allocation
```

### Online Commission Distribution

For the standard online affiliate model, confirmed partner commission is distributed as follows:

```text
Confirmed Partner Commission
              ↓
        ┌─────┴─────┐
        ↓           ↓
       30%         70%
        ↓           ↓
   iSmartBonus    Customer
     Revenue      Bonus Allocation
```

For example, if an online partner generates a confirmed $10 commission:

```text
Confirmed Commission:    $10
iSmartBonus Revenue:       $3
Customer Bonus:            $7
```

There is no physical QR flow and no immediate iSmartBonus checkout discount in this model.

Purchase attribution and commission confirmation are handled separately by the corresponding online partner or affiliate integration.

## Business Model

iSmartBonus supports multiple merchant participation models.

### Performance-Based Model

Businesses fund rewards when eligible purchases occur.

The distribution model depends on the commerce channel.

For physical merchants, the standard commission distribution is:

- 25% — immediate customer discount;
- 25% — iSmartBonus revenue;
- 50% — customer bonus allocation.

For online affiliate partners, the standard confirmed commission distribution is:

- 30% — iSmartBonus revenue;
- 70% — customer bonus allocation.

### SaaS Model

Businesses can also participate through a fixed platform subscription model while their loyalty budget is directed toward customer bonuses.

This gives merchants flexibility in how they participate in the ecosystem while keeping the customer experience unified.

## Bonus Lifecycle

After a qualifying transaction, the allocated bonus follows the platform's lifecycle rules.

The current backend implements an initial lock period followed by progressive availability.

```text
Bonus Created
     ↓
Initial Lock Period
     ↓
Progressive Availability
     ↓
Fully Available
     ↓
Eligible Withdrawal
```

The current lifecycle implementation includes:

- an initial period during which the bonus is locked;
- progressive availability after the initial lock period;
- full availability after the defined lifecycle period;
- validation of withdrawal eligibility.

Bonus lifecycle logic is separated from purchase processing so that the same lifecycle system can be used for bonuses originating from different commerce channels.

---

## Scarcity Mechanics

The broader iSmartBonus model includes bonus and token rules designed to connect platform activity with digital asset scarcity.

These can include:

- bonus locking periods;
- early-unlock rules;
- burn mechanics where applicable;
- withdrawal limits linked to verified customer spending.

Some blockchain settlement and burn operations are currently handled manually and are not represented in this repository as automated on-chain functionality.

The objective is to connect the digital asset economy to verified commercial activity rather than simply issuing unlimited traditional reward points.

---

## Withdrawals

Once bonus value becomes eligible, it can enter the iSmartBonus withdrawal request flow.

The backend currently contains withdrawal request logic for:

- on-chain settlement requests;
- cash pickup requests;
- withdrawal status tracking;
- duplicate and request validation;
- pickup verification data.

```text
Eligible Bonus
      ↓
Withdrawal Request
      ↓
Validation
      ↓
Pending
      ↓
Administrative Verification
      ↓
Final Settlement
```

Final blockchain settlement is currently handled separately from the automated WordPress backend.

---

## Current Blockchain Settlement

ISB is the Solana-based digital asset layer of the iSmartBonus ecosystem.

At the current stage, final blockchain settlement operations are performed manually after the corresponding platform transaction has been verified.

The backend currently handles:

- purchase verification;
- commission distribution;
- bonus calculation;
- bonus lifecycle logic;
- withdrawal requests.

Final blockchain operations are executed separately.

Future development will automate the settlement layer and connect the backend directly with Solana infrastructure.

This repository does not represent manual blockchain operations as automated on-chain functionality.

## Source Modules

### Signed Offline Partner QR

`src/offline-partners/signed-partner-qr.php`

Contains the signed QR logic used for purchases at physical merchant locations.

The flow includes:

- user identification;
- unique token generation;
- timestamp validation;
- HMAC-SHA256 signatures;
- signature verification;
- expiration validation;
- protection against modified QR payloads.

This module is specifically designed for physical merchants.

Online marketplaces and affiliate partners use separate attribution mechanisms.

---

### Offline Purchase Engine

`src/purchases/offline-purchase-engine.php`

Handles verified physical merchant purchases.

Responsibilities include:

- customer validation;
- merchant validation;
- signed QR verification;
- duplicate transaction protection;
- merchant commission calculation;
- offline 25% / 25% / 50% distribution;
- transaction storage.

---

### Online Purchase Engine

`src/purchases/online-purchase-engine.php`

Handles normalized transactions originating from online and affiliate partners.

Responsibilities include:

- customer and partner validation;
- external transaction identification;
- duplicate transaction protection;
- confirmed commission processing;
- 30% / 70% commission distribution;
- transaction storage.

Partner-specific attribution and reporting integrations remain separate from this processing layer.

---

### Bonus Lifecycle Engine

`src/bonuses/bonus-lifecycle-engine.php`

Handles the lifecycle of bonus value after a qualifying transaction.

Responsibilities include:

- initial lock validation;
- transaction-age calculation;
- progressive bonus availability;
- full availability calculation;
- withdrawal eligibility validation.

---

### Withdrawal Engine

`src/withdrawals/withdrawal-engine.php`

Handles withdrawal request processing after bonus value becomes eligible.

Responsibilities include:

- withdrawal method validation;
- eligible order selection;
- withdrawal request creation;
- on-chain destination information;
- cash pickup requests;
- verification data;
- withdrawal status management.

Final settlement is handled separately.
